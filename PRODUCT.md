# Product

## Register

product

## Users

A 10-person team at Amigo Tech that splits its week between home and office. Each person opens the schedule from a Slack link, often on a phone, usually on Friday afternoon when the reminder arrives, to pick up to two home days for the coming week and to see who else is home.

## Product Purpose

Spread office presence evenly across the week. Each person marks their home days within per-day limits and per-person rules (one Monday and one Friday a month, no Monday+Friday in the same week, no Friday followed by Monday, at most two days a week). The screen also shows which parking cards are free each day. Success: the team fills the schedule every week without friction, and no office day ends up empty or crowded by accident.

## Brand Personality

Amigo Tech's own identity: the company design system (blue primary, blue-tinted grays, green accents, system font). Friendly, clear, quick. An internal tool that feels like part of the company's product family, not a generic template.

## Anti-references

Generic AI or template look: identical icon cards, gradient accents, hero metrics, glassy panels. No preferred external reference app.

## Design Principles

- The answer at a glance: who is home, how many spots are left, and what I can still pick, without reading paragraphs.
- Explain every "no": a blocked day always says why, in plain Portuguese.
- Thumb-first: the full flow (pick name, tap days, confirm) works with one hand on a 360px phone.
- Company family: reuse the Amigo design system colors and type before inventing new ones.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Visible focus on every control. State never depends on color alone: selected, full and blocked also carry text or icons. Respect reduced motion.
